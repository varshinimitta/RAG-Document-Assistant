TechNova Solutions is a software company founded in 2015.

The company is headquartered in Hyderabad, India.

TechNova Solutions develops artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and data analytics solutions.

The company has more than 500 employees.

Its main products include an AI customer support platform, a cloud monitoring system, and a data analytics platform.

The company provides services to businesses in healthcare, finance, retail, and education.

The company's working hours are Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM.
